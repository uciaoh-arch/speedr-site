隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-10-22。開發者：Yu Chiao Huang。

我們蒐集什麼

speedr 本身不要求註冊，也不蒐集、不儲存您的姓名、聯絡方式或定位紀錄。為免費提供 App，我們使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告，廣告服務會蒐集下列資料（詳見下方「廣告」）。

廣告

App 在停車或低速時顯示 Google AdMob 廣告。AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼（IDFA）、大致位置、廣告互動，以及當機、效能與其他診斷資料，用於投放與衡量廣告、分析與防止詐騙。若您在 iOS 的「追蹤」提示選擇不允許，AdMob 不會取得 IDFA，只會顯示非個人化廣告，App 全部功能照常使用。您可隨時於 iPhone「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「追蹤」變更。Google 如何處理資料請見 Google 隱私權政策。

定位資料

speedr 僅在您的裝置上使用定位來計算與測速點的距離與方向，不會將定位資料傳給我們。廣告服務為投放廣告可能取得大致位置。背景定位由您自行選擇開啟，可隨時在 iPhone 設定中關閉。

本機設定

語言、提醒距離等偏好設定僅儲存在您的裝置。

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-22. Developer: Yu Chiao Huang.

What we collect

speedr itself requires no account and does not collect or store your name, contact details or location history. To keep the app free we show Google AdMob ads, and the ad service collects the data described below.

Advertising

The app shows Google AdMob ads while you are stopped or moving slowly. AdMob may collect device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate location, ad interactions, and crash, performance and other diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. If you decline the iOS tracking prompt, AdMob does not receive the IDFA and shows non-personalized ads; every feature keeps working. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Location

speedr uses location only on your device to calculate distance and direction to cameras and does not send it to us. The ad service may obtain approximate location to serve ads. Background location is opt-in and can be turned off any time in iPhone Settings.

Local preferences

Language and alert-distance preferences are stored only on your device.
