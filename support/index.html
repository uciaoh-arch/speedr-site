speedr 支援

speedr 是 iPhone 測速提醒 App，只提醒行進方向前方的測速點。

常見問題

  • 沒有收到提醒？請確認已允許定位，並開啟「精確位置」；車速超過 10 km/h 後才會辨識行駛方向。
  • 鎖定螢幕後沒有提醒？需在設定開啟背景提醒，並將定位權限設為「永遠」。
  • 資料來源？內政部警政署及各縣市警察局公開的測速與科技執法設置地點（政府資料開放授權條款第1版），實際執法以現場標示為準。

speedr Support

speedr is an iPhone speed camera alert app that only warns about cameras ahead in your direction of travel.

FAQ

  • No alerts? Allow location access and enable Precise Location. Travel direction is detected once you exceed 10 km/h.
  • No alerts when locked? Enable background alerts in Settings and set location access to Always.
  • Data source? Speed and enforcement camera locations published by the National Police Agency and county/city police (Open Government Data License v1). Always follow posted signs on the road.
